# Changelog

All notable changes to Sleep Tracker will be documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- Naps: a sleep that ends between 10am and 9pm and lasts under 4 hours is saved as a nap. Naps show in the Log but stay out of your averages, usual bedtime, charts, calendar and week bars
- Mark any sleep as a nap or a night from its long-press menu, or with the Nap switch when editing it
- The Log shows bed and wake times under every sleep
- Long-press the quick tile to open the app

### Changed
- The "Last night" card is now "Previous sleep" and shows your latest sleep, nap or night
- While you sleep, the quick tile shows how long you've been asleep ("Sleeping · 6h 12m") instead of when you went to bed, and keeps counting while the shade is open

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added
- First release of Sleep Tracker
- Tap the moon at bedtime and again when you wake; hold it to cancel a session started by mistake
- Tonight: usual bedtime, bed and wake times, last night's sleep and the past 7 days at a glance
- Log: a timeline grouped by week, and a month calendar shaded by how close you came to your goal
- Insights: averages, longest and shortest sleep, bedtime consistency and a bed and wake chart
- Rate each night with stars and add notes, at wake-up or any time later
- Add, edit or delete any night; long-press anything to see its details
- Quick settings tile to start and stop sleep without opening the app
- Back up your data to a file and restore it from Settings
- Sleep goal, light and dark themes
